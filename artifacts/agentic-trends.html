04 / Working thesis

The new design brief

Three questions now matter more than “which model is smartest?”

Can it reach the right context?

Build for the agent’s real surface area: tools, data, identity and the protocols that connect them.

Can it prove the work?

Make evaluation part of the loop — acceptance tests, references, traces, and human review where judgment matters.

Can it earn more agency?

Use graduated permissions and customer-controlled data so autonomy expands with demonstrated reliability.
